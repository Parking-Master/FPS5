Chat Rules

If you're discovering FPS5 for the first time and want to use the World Chat, please take a minute to review the Chatroom Rules, which help keep everyone safe and respective while chatting.

Be kind!

Talk to others how you'd talk to them in real life. Leave out negativity, such as discussing world problems or politics. Be kind and respectful to everyone.

No bad messages

We are completely against innaproppriate messages and profanity, so don't even think about sending messages like that. Repeated violation of this rule will result in account deletion and IP blocking. To see everything we prohibit in messages, check out our Terms & Conditions.

No spamming

If you're just testing out the chat for the first time, saying "eeee" is no problem. Just don't repeatedly spam the same thing, post in languages other than English, or post random strings of characters that don't make any sense.

Invite players

FPS5 gives you the ability to create custom lobbies with all sorts of different options. After you set up a lobby, you can invite other players to join it from the world chat, by either clicking the button in the message box, or clicking a player and selecting Invite to Lobby. So invite players to your games and have fun!
